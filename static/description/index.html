Complete Step-by-Step Workflow

A safe, transparent, and non-destructive 4-step synchronization process.

1
Download Template

Open the wizard and choose your template format:

  • App Permission Matrix: Auto-generates columns for all active modules (Sales, Inventory, Accounting, etc.) with Excel dropdowns.
  • Role Assignment Matrix: Standard format for assigning predefined role names.
2
Upload & Scan

Upload your completed spreadsheet and click "Muat & Cek Preview Data".

  • Smart case-insensitive matching by Email, Login, or Full Name.
  • Empty rows and formatting artifacts are automatically ignored.
3
Verify & Select

Review the interactive verification table before applying changes.

  • Toggle individual rows with Pilih / Sync switches.
  • Use quick buttons: "Pilih Semua" or "Batal Pilih".
  • Live counters show Total, Selected, Matched, and Missing users.
4
Apply & Enforce

Click "Konfirmasi & Terapkan Role" to finalize synchronization.

  • Creates/updates entries in res.users.role.line.
  • Executes set_groups_from_roles to activate security groups immediately.

Comprehensive Feature Highlights

Granular Row Selection

Selectively choose which rows to sync. Unchecked users are completely untouched, allowing partial or staged rollouts.

Dynamic Template Generator

Exports ready-to-use Excel templates with built-in dropdown validators that dynamically query the modules installed in your current database.

Multi-Strategy User Matching

Flexible resolution by username prefix, full email, or employee name. Cleanly handles case differences and whitespace.

Auto-Create Missing Roles

Automatically provisions new roles in res.users.role with baseline Internal User rights whenever unknown roles appear.

Instant Group Application

Triggers standard OCA security recalculation, ensuring menu items, record rules, and ACLs take effect immediately.
