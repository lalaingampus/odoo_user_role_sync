To use this module:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Users & Companies > Sync Roles from Excel**.
2. **Step 1 (Upload & Options):** 
   - (Optional) Click **Download Excel Template** to get an auto-generated spreadsheet tailored to your installed Odoo apps.
   - Select your `.xlsx` file and choose sync settings (e.g. *Auto-create Missing Roles*).
   - Click **Muat & Cek Preview Data**.
3. **Step 2 (Preview & Selection):**
   - Review matched vs missing users in the interactive preview table.
   - Use the **Pilih / Sync** switches or the **Pilih Semua / Batal Pilih** buttons to select only the users you want to sync.
   - Click **Konfirmasi & Terapkan Role**.
4. **Step 3 (Result & Verification):**
   - Review the final execution summary.
   - Click **Lihat Data di Users (Tab User Roles)** to inspect the updated users directly.
